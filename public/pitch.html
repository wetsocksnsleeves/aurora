lightshow

Lightshows from synchronised phone screens

Open on the animation. One line: Lightshow turns the audience's own phones into a stadium light show, with no app and no hardware.
The problem

Crowd light shows need hardware

  • LED wristbands: bought, handed out, thrown away.
  • Phone torches: free, but out of sync.
  • Phone screens: full colour, already in every pocket.
Wristband shows look great but cost money and logistics for every event. The screens are already in the room.
The idea

Lots of things, moving together

Drone light shows

Hundreds of drones, one picture in the sky.

Stadium waves

Thousands of people, one motion around the ground.

It's satisfying when everything moves as one.

Finding your seat

The phone locates itself with its camera

  • The big screen shows a target: four anchors around an LED-style grille.
  • The phone matches ~300 points and solves where the camera is in the room.
  • Field of view is measured from the angle, or from a slow sweep using the gyro.
The grille tells the phone which corner is which. Far back, where the grille is too small to read, it falls back to the four anchors.
Keeping time

One clock across hundreds of phones

  • Cristian's algorithm: ping the server, keep the last 40 samples, trust the one with the lowest round trip.
  • Corrections slew at 150 ms/s, so nothing visibly jumps mid-show.
  • Every frame reads the clock and looks up its colour. A late frame still shows the right colour.
